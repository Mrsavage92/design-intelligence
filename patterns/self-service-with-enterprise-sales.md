# Self-service entry with an enterprise sales path

## When applicable

Products serving both small teams able to start independently and organizations needing assisted purchasing. Confirm actual customer needs and sales operations before applying.

## Supporting findings

[Dated pricing observations](../research-findings/20261025-saas-pricing-integration.md) record three independently operated original product pages: [Linear](https://linear.app/pricing), [Slack](https://slack.com/pricing) and [Notion](https://www.notion.com/pricing), accessed 2026-10-25 with live text retrieval. Each pairs self-service entry with an enterprise contact path. E2 applies only to recurrence of this content convention, not causality, accessibility compliance or target-product performance.

## Counterexamples

The same finding records Notion's audience-grouped content and Slack's offer terms. Products with only a sales-assisted motion or only a self-service audience may not need both paths. No universal card count or layout follows from this sample.

## Accessibility and mobile risks

Differentiate action labels and destination semantics. Test keyboard access, readable plan comparisons and mobile presentation in the actual implementation; none was tested here.

## Original implementation approach

Choose tier summaries and actions from the target product's eligibility, capabilities and buying process. Do not reuse competitor copy, assets or economics. Keep limitations and billing commitments clear.

## How to validate in target product

Start with user comprehension tasks and an instrumented baseline. Define plan-choice completion and confusion/refund guardrails before an experiment. No conversion uplift or E4 validation exists for this pattern. Recheck the linked sources before consequential reuse.
